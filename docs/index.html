📊 Detailed Tanking Statistics & Acronyms Glossary (WotLK 3.3.5a)

Acronym / Stat Full Name & Cap Mechanical Description & WotLK 3.3.5a Formula
DEF Defense Skill
Cap: 540 (Raid) / 535 (HC)		 Warrior's defensive skill rating (+140 Defense Rating beyond the 400 base at Lv.80). Achieving 540 Defense Skill (equivalent to 689 Defense Rating) reduces the chance of receiving critical melee strikes from Level 83 Raid Bosses by 5.60%, achieving absolute "Crit Immunity".
ARM / Armor Effective Armor & Contextual DR%
Hard Cap: 75.0% DR		 Effective armor encompassing all active modifiers (+25% Inspiration / Ancestral Fortitude, Stoneskin, Devotion Aura) and debuffs (Sunder Armor, Acid Spit, Faerie Fire). Physical Damage Reduction (DR%) is dynamic and contextual to the target:
• Default / Character Sheet (Lv.80): Blizzard constant K = 15232.5 → 25,253 armor yields 62.4% (matching the 62.38% shown on the in-game character sheet tooltip).
• Raid Boss (Lv.83 / Skull): Blizzard constant K = 16635 → 25,253 armor yields 60.3%, indicating true mitigation against raid bosses (Lich King, Halion).
CTC Combat Table Coverage
Uncrushable Cap: 102.4%		 Total single-roll combat table coverage in World of Warcraft:
CTC = Dodge% + Parry% + Block% + Boss Miss%
Against a Lv.83 Boss (+0.6% melee hit bonus), reaching 102.4% CTC categorically excludes normal unmitigated hits: every single incoming swing is dodged, parried, blocked with the shield, or misses. Incorporates non-stacking boss debuffs (Insect Swarm / Scorpid Sting max +3%).
SBV Shield Block Value
Shield Block Absorption		 Fixed amount of physical damage subtracted from each blocked melee swing. Beyond passive/active mitigation (doubled by Shield Block), Shield Block Value is the cardinal attribute scaling Shield Slam and Damage Shield damage and threat.
Hit & Exp Hit Rating & Expertise
Soft Cap: 8% Hit | 26 Exp		 Melee Hit Cap (8.0% / 263 rating): Eliminates misses on melee auto-attacks and special abilities (including Taunt; 7.0% for Alliance with Draenei Heroic Presence).
Expertise Cap (26 Exp / 214 rating soft cap, 56 hard cap): 26 Expertise completely eliminates boss frontal Dodges. 56 Expertise eliminates frontal Parries, vital to prevent Parry-Haste on bosses that support it (e.g. Lady Deathwhisper, Sindragosa, Halion).
Avoid Pure Avoidance
Dodge + Parry + Miss		 Combined share of attacks where the tank completely avoids impact, suffering zero physical damage. Unlike Block (which mitigates a flat amount equal to SBV), pure avoidance completely negates the attack.
EHP Effective Health Pool
EHP = HP / (1 - DR%)		 Theoretical raw physical damage pool a tank can absorb consecutively before dying without external heals or absorbs. Primary survival metric for enduring Tank Busters (such as Soul Reaper on Lich King or Impale on Gormok).

🛡️ Architecture, Load Conditions & Combat Safety

✨ Exclusive Load Condition [Devastate]
The entire WeakAuras suite is bound to the exclusive talent [Devastate] (Talent ID 103, Protection tree Tier 8 Column 1). The suite compiles and loads into WoW memory only when the warrior has this talent active, guaranteeing zero CPU waste and zero overlap when playing Arms, Fury, or PvP specs.
